## Intro
This year I have gone all in with my gardening, pushing my balcony to its limits. 
## The balcony
The balcony is split up in two parts, one part is a small greenhouse that me and my dad built in modular parts so it is easy to set up and take down. 
It measures in at around 250cm x 150cm.

The other part is "regular balcony", but with some containers and a raised bed I built with some material from a pallet that I saved from being thrown away by someone.
## The plants
I start all my plants from seed, except for some strawberries I planted in a cheap terracotta "tower" (big mistake).

Currently the plant crew consists of:
### In the Greenhouse
**Three** indeterminate tomatoes all planted in grow bags
- *Gardener's Delight*  (Cherry Tomato) 
- *Black Cherry*  (Cherry Tomato)
- *Brandywine Red*  (Beefstake)

**Six** determinate "dwarf bush tomatoes"
- All *Tiny Tim*

**Four** cucumbers,
- *Iznik* F1
- *Baby* F1
- *Party Time* F1
- *Passandra* F1

**Two** Chili Padron and a snacking pepper *Snacking Purple* variety.
### The rest of the balcony
This space is a combination of some really prolific specimens and a lot of shame.
While the three biggest tomato plants lives here, the same *three-musketeer-combo* that's in the greenhouse, but instead of being planted in one (too small to be honest) grow bag each, they're all planted in the big home-made-pallet-raised bad mentioned earlier.

There are usually some salad varieties going on, some herbs rotating. 
There's also a kale *Autumn Star* F1 and a broccoli *Summer Purple*. There might be a broccolini going on, but I mixed up the labels at some point so I really have no idea.

And for the part that I'm ashamed of, there's the rest of all the tomato seeds I started that survived, didn't fit anywhere but I didn't have the heart to throw out.
I don't know how many there are, but they consist of the three earlier mentioned indeterminate varieties spread out where there's space.
I would estimate around 15 plants spread out.
