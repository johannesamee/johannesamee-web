My collection of [Ryobi ONE+](https://uk.ryobitools.eu/one-plus/) 18V cordless tools. All on the same battery platform.

## Power Tools

- **[RPD18X1-0](https://uk.ryobitools.eu/power-tools/drilling-and-screwdriving/combi-drill/rpd18x/rpd18x-0/)** --- 18V ONE+ HP Brushless Combi Drill. 2-speed, 24 torque settings, 13mm keyless chuck. Drill, hammer drill, and screwdriver modes.

- **[RMT18X-0](https://uk.ryobitools.eu/power-tools/multi-tools/multi-tools/rmt18x/rmt18x-1/)** --- 18V ONE+ HP Brushless Multi Tool. 10,000--20,000 OPM oscillating tool for cutting, sanding, and scraping. Quick-release blade swap.

## Cleaning

- **[RY18PWX41A-0](https://www.ryobi.com.au/products/pressure-washers/18v-one-hptm-brushless-power-washer-tool-only)** --- 18V ONE+ HP Brushless Power Washer. Up to 41 bar / 480 PSI. Three pressure settings and 3-in-1 nozzle. Includes 6m syphon kit for bucket or lake water.

- **[RHV18-0](https://www.diy.com/departments/ryobi-one-18v-cordless-0-6l-hand-held-vacuum-cleaner-bare-tool-rhv18-0/4892210204844_BQ.prd)** --- 18V ONE+ Cordless Hand Vac. 600ml dust bowl, HEPA 11 filter, up to 34 minutes runtime with 5.0Ah battery.

## Lighting

- **[RSL18-0](https://www.amazon.co.uk/RYOBI-RSL18-0-Cordless-Stick-Light/dp/B0F48YBGDG)** --- 18V ONE+ LED Stick Light. 24 LEDs, up to 800 lumens, three brightness modes. 325-degree pivoting lens and hanging hook.
